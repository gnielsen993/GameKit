Ten ways to hold play.

This is a strategic exploration, not final production artwork. Each route tests a different piece of the 2.0 story. The strongest mark should recognize at 32px, feel like a collection rather than one game, and support light, dark, and tinted variants without losing its idea.

Recommended synthesis

Combine Play Quilt’s modular collection with Quiet Fan’s unmistakable drawer slot. Keep the palette warm, use one chosen accent, and test the silhouette in monochrome before polishing color.
