# GameDrawer: a small break without the ad break

Prepared 2026-09-08 for 1.6. Editorial hypothesis, not measured growth.

## The person and the moment

Someone who already likes Sudoku, Solitaire or quick word puzzles, and wants a few minutes of play without an ad, a currency balance or a required sign-up. A second useful moment is playing a familiar game alongside a show or stream. Travelers and commuters get the additional benefit of offline play.

The job is immediate: find a game I recognize, start playing, and return later if I have to stop. The download should feel easier to justify than collecting eleven separate apps.

## The pitch

**Your favorites. Without the ads.**

Eleven familiar logic, card, word and arcade games. Play offline, get a useful next step when stuck, and make room for your video.

Current access: every game is free to play, with no subscription or in-app currency. This is the current offer, not a promise about all future pricing or advertising. Revenue optimization is out of this campaign; visibility and first-time downloads are the objectives.

## What changes from the old pitch

Lead with the recognizable games and the relief from ad breaks. "Local-first", "SwiftData", "DesignKit", board-generation details and raw feature counts belong behind the benefit, if anywhere. Video Mode stays a meaningful difference, but it should not make someone think they need a video to use the app.

The live listing reviewed on September 8 names Connections although it is absent from the app. The new roster fixes that mismatch. It also replaces generic "improved performance" update language with what an existing player can do in 1.6.

## Success and measurement

Use App Store Connect's aggregate reporting rather than adding an analytics SDK to the game. Before changing the live page, save a 28-day baseline by storefront and source: search impressions, product-page views, first-time downloads and available conversion metrics. Preserve the exact ASC metric definitions; do not mix total downloads, redownloads and first-time downloads.

Track first-time downloads as the primary outcome, with search visibility and conversion as separate diagnostic measures. The first campaign changes metadata and screenshots together, so treat before/after results as directional, not causal. Account for the 1.6 launch and any promotion.

After an initial comparable period, test one screenshot opener at a time using Product Page Optimization if eligible and traffic supports a useful sample. Keep the rest of the sequence fixed. Do not declare a winner after a handful of downloads or invent confidence intervals. Keep a dated experiment log and the exact assets used.

## Search hypothesis

Recommended name: GameDrawer: Offline Games.
Subtitle: Sudoku, Solitaire & Puzzles.
Keywords add the other actual games and related vocabulary without repeating the name/subtitle. "Offline" and "no ads" appear in comparable public listings; that confirms audience vocabulary, not search volume or achievable rank. Public competitor names are evidence only and are not placed in our keyword field.

Retain the existing Casual category initially so the first listing refresh does not also change every discovery variable. Review Puzzle as a game subcategory against actual ASC options and subsequent search data. No console category change is claimed here.
