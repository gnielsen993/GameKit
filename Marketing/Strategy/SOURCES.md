# Research and evidence boundaries

Checked 2026-09-08. The strategy is an editorial recommendation; no paid keyword tool, search-volume dataset or App Store Connect analytics was accessed.

- [Apple product-page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/): name/subtitle metadata, keyword selection, promotional text and product proof. Keywords have a 100-character limit; avoid duplicate terms. Promotional text is not a keyword-ranking field.
- [Apple screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/): 1–10 screenshots per display size; this set uses accepted 1320×2868 iPhone and 2064×2752 iPad portrait canvases.
- [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), 2.3.3: show the app in use. Our screenshots are captured from the real app and remain immutable during composition.
- [Current GameDrawer listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gamedrawer-classic-games/id6765536382): observed v1.5, Free, subtitle "Calm logic puzzles", Casual, and an inaccurate Connections roster entry. No public ratings overview was shown. Do not infer downloads or review sentiment from its absence.
- [No Wifi Mini Games listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/no-wifi-mini-games/id6479628876): an example of offline/ad-free vocabulary in this market. Its advertised game count is not independently audited and is not borrowed as a claim for GameDrawer.

Local primary evidence: GameDescriptor's eleven-game roster; 1.6 closure unit/UI tests; native marketing captures and source hashes. Demo stats come from the app's existing ScreenshotSeeder on isolated simulators, not from real customers.
