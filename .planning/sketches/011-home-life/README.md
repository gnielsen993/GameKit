# Sketch 011: Where does the life come from

## Design Question
Gabe's reaction to sketch 010 (2026-11-26): the navigation is fine but there is
no life in the app. It needs a balance between clean and undistracting, and
somewhere people want to be. Color cannot carry that, because the player picks
the preset. So what does?

## How to View
```
open .planning/sketches/011-home-life/index.html
```
Switch direction, then switch theme preset, then turn animations off.

## Variants
- **A: The games are alive** — tiles are small real boards drawn from the theme, not icons. One at a time plays a few moves, like an arcade cabinet in attract mode. Saved boards show your actual position. Tapping a tile grows it into the board.
- **B: Today's page** — Home is laid out like a newspaper puzzle page: a date, today's puzzle as the lead, unfinished boards as ink diagrams. Nothing moves; it is alive because it changes daily.
- **C: Pieces in a tray** — games are chunky pieces sitting in a tray that drop in, sink when pressed and spring back. Rounded type, saturated color, real depth.

## What to Look For
- Which one would you open the app just to look at, and which would you tire of in a week?
- Under Dracula and Voltage, which still looks like itself?
- With animations off, which still has something left? A and C lose their main device; B loses nothing.
- A puts one design language on Home and in the games. B and C keep Home distinct from the game screens.
- C's depth conflicts with the Classic restomod rule in CLAUDE.md §1 (no skeuomorphic depth). Choosing it means changing that rule.

## Notes
Mini boards are illustrative start positions and scripted moves, not engine
output. All three directions reuse them so the comparison is about treatment.
