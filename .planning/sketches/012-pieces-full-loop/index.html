Less, with weight

  1. One thing first. Home leads with a single action.
  2. Everything you press is a piece. Same shape on Home, in the pad, on the win.
  3. Every move answers back. A placed number pops; a finished row lights up.
  4. The game is the decoration. Faces are boards, and one plays at a time.
  5. Endings are rewarded. The time counts up and the next game is one tap.
