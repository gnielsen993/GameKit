# Sketch 012: Pieces, the full loop

## Design Question
After sketch 011 Gabe chose C's look and wanted A's aliveness with it. He then
reframed "life": it is not movement, it is whatever makes successful apps land,
and the brief is "less is more, but not nothing". This sketch states that as
five principles and tests them on one playable loop instead of another Home.

## How to View
```
open .planning/sketches/012-pieces-full-loop/index.html
```
Press Resume, play a few numbers, then use "3 cells from a win" to reach the ending.

## The language: less, with weight
1. **One thing first.** Home leads with a single action.
2. **Everything you press is a piece.** One shape on Home, in the number pad and on the win sheet.
3. **Every move answers back.** A placed number pops; a completed row, column or box lights up in sequence.
4. **The game is the decoration.** Piece faces are boards; in the tray one plays a move at a time.
5. **Endings are rewarded.** The time counts up, a best is named, the next puzzle is one tap.

Where each came from: 1, 3 and 5 are the transferable parts of what large
casual games do (instant play, feedback on every action, a rewarding end). 2 is
the existing DESIGN.md §3.0 rule "depth marks interactivity", turned up until
it is felt. 4 is what stops the app reading as a folder of eleven icons. The
loud skin of those games is deliberately not adopted.

## What is a single variant here
One direction, not three. The comparison is against the shipped 1.6 screens.

## What to Look For
- Is one action on Home enough, or does it now feel empty? ("Less is more, but not nothing.")
- Do the pad keys feel like the same object as the Home pieces?
- Is the row-complete answer satisfying or noisy after the tenth time?
- Does the win feel earned without shouting?
- Dracula and Voltage: does it still look like itself?
- Animations off: pieces and color remain, pops and waves go. Is what is left acceptable?

## Not covered
Stats, Minesweeper and the card games. Sudoku's Value/Notes toggle, erase and
assist are omitted to keep the loop readable; they would be pieces too.
