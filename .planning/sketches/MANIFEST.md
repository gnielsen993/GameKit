# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
"My small game collection, ready when I am." Calm, direct, no engagement
pressure. Sketches use the Chrome Diner look of the shipped app (cream paper,
white surfaces, diner-red accent, colored game tiles) so reactions are about
structure. `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` are the authority; sketches do not
introduce a new visual world.

## Reference Points
The shipped 1.6 Home (`assets/screenshots/home-game-drawer.png`).
