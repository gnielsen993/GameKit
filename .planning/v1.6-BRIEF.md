**Status:** brief (pre-milestone) · **Created:** 2026-07-31
**One-liner:** Stop the app quietly punishing people, then teach the two
games where a deduction can be honestly explained.

## Implementation amendment — 2026-08-05 playtest

The original survey below was a useful conservative starting point, but the
implemented pilots exposed a different product problem: help was hidden, its
copy was too technical, and Nonogram highlighted several squares without
telling the player whether to fill or mark them. The following decisions
supersede conflicting statements later in this brief:

- Assistance ships in all six piece-by-piece games where a stuck state can be
  helped honestly: Minesweeper, Nonogram, Sudoku, Word Grid, Five Letter, and
  FreeCell. Stack, Snake, Merge, and Klondike remain out of assist scope.
- Normal play gets a visible lightbulb toolbar button. Video Mode keeps a
  visible compact lightbulb in its control row or repositioned toolbar; it
  does not depend on a settings menu that may disappear in compact layouts.
- The shared coach card uses plain action copy, an optional progress count,
  one direct action, and a dismiss action. Timers pause while it is open. The
  card reserves space opposite the Video Mode window and never covers the
  board target it is explaining.
- Gameplay choices no longer use stock system alerts or action sheets. Resume,
  abandon, size, difficulty, and new-deal decisions use the shared themed
  choice card; completed-game New puzzle / New board actions stay immediate.
- Settings support, reset, import-error, and account-deletion feedback also
  appears as themed inline cards, leaving no stock alert/action-sheet surfaces.
- Nonogram distinguishes **Fill** from **mark X** on the board. Highlights
  persist and clear one-by-one only as the requested actions are completed.
- Cell reveals are allowed as a fallback when an engine cannot produce an
  honest next deduction. They are never the default or a metered resource.
- When Nonogram or Sudoku reaches zero lives, the loss is recorded once and
  the timer freezes. **Keep Solving** preserves the board in Practice, gives
  corrective feedback without further penalties, and never writes a second
  win/loss result.

## Why this milestone

This is Release Slice 4 ("Be Kind pilots") from `v2.0-VISION.md:241`,
scoped from evidence rather than from the vision doc's guess. A per-game
survey on 2026-07-31 asked one question of all ten games — *how do we best
help a stuck player here?* — and returned a result that reshapes the slice:

**Two-thirds of the available kindness is defect repair, not new features.**
Six games already punish players through bugs. Fixing those is cheaper, less
debatable, and more on-brand than any hint that could ship alongside them.

The survey also killed several assumptions worth recording:

- **A general "lifesaver" / revive is rejected.** Where lives exist, a
  no-stakes mode already sits next to them — `SudokuGameMode.swift`
  documents `.free` as "wrong placements never lock the cell or fail the
  session." A revive erases a choice the player deliberately made.
  In Minesweeper it is worse than neutral: a mine hit *is* information, so
  undoing it converts a loss into a free mine reveal and makes random
  clicking rational.
- **Reveal-a-cell is the wrong primary assist.** It teaches nothing and is
  the closest shape in the design space to the per-use currency the vision
  bans.
- **Talkthrough is achievable**, but only where a rule trace exists to
  narrate. It is not a universal answer (see Scope §3 and Out of Scope).

**Supersedes `milestones/v1.2.1-hints.md`** (v1.2.1 Hints, 2026-05-22,
never committed). That sketch proposed reveal-a-cell across three games on a
3-per-puzzle allowance. Both halves are now rejected: reveal-a-cell for the
reason above, and a resetting allowance because a rationed pool is a soft
currency with the serial numbers filed off. Assists here are unmetered and
free. Mark that file superseded when this milestone opens.

## Scope — what ships in v1.6

### 0. Record semantics (gates everything else)

Decided 2026-08-01 — see "Decided — record semantics" below for the three
rulings and their reasoning. This section is now an implementation list, and
it gates the pilots (not the dignity fixes, which touch no assist field).
The work exists because `GameStats.record` evaluates `BestTime` /
`BestScore` blindly on any win.

- `GameRecord` gains `assistCount: Int? = nil`. Optional is mandatory —
  CloudKit rejects required properties on an existing schema.
- Export envelope `schemaVersion` bump; `StatsExporter` passthrough.
- **The rule:** an assisted win is a win. Games played, win rate, and
  streaks all count it. It does not write a best time or best score.
- `SettingsStore.assistsEnabled` — the purist off-switch required by the
  vision's principle 5. Mind the default-true `object(forKey:)` caveat.
- Every save-state blob that gains an assist field needs a default, or
  in-flight saves silently drop on decode.

### 1. Dignity fixes — the punishing defects

These are bug fixes. They need no product debate and should land first.

1. **FiveLetter's daily word is not deterministic.**
   `FiveLetterViewModel.swift:278` selects via `abs(id.hashValue) %
   answers.count`. Swift seeds `String.hashValue` per process, so the daily
   answer changes on relaunch, and differs across devices and users. It only
   appears stable once a first guess writes a save. Needs a stable hash
   (FNV/SHA over the date string). **This also blocks Slice 3 (Daily).**
2. **Sudoku Free mode accepts wrong digits with no feedback.** `commitValue`
   commits unconditionally in `.free`; the comment at
   `SudokuViewModel.swift:354-356` and the `SudokuGameMode.swift` header
   both claim a red solution-mismatch overlay that was never built. Players
   can grind indefinitely on a provably dead board.
3. **Nonogram Free mode, identically** — `applyMutation` only consults the
   solution inside the `.lives` branch. Worse: the contradiction is already
   computed and discarded. `crossOffMask` returns all-false on an
   inconsistent line (`Engine/NonogramHints.swift:157-161`) and the UI
   renders that the same as "nothing crossed off yet."
4. **Klondike + FreeCell undo dies on resume** — history is wiped on restore,
   and `canUndo` requires `.playing`, so undo switches off exactly when the
   player is stuck. FreeCell throws away an unbounded history.
5. **Both record the loss before the player accepts it.** Premature today;
   a double-counted deal (one loss *and* one win) the moment undo outlives
   terminal state. Move the write to the point the dead end is accepted.
6. **Merge shows a phantom best score** — `viewModel.bestScore` is
   session-local and never seeded from `GameStats.bestScore(gameKind:mode:)`.
7. **Arcade run-end** — both banners pass `subtitle: nil` though the field
   exists, and Stack has no best-score awareness anywhere. The v1.5 spec
   (`FEATURES.md:96,128`) asked for a run summary with a PB indicator.

### 2. Retry this board

A cross-game loss-banner change, and the honest alternative to a revive.
The loss is recorded normally and the run is over — but the player replays
the *same* board instead of being handed a new random one. No degeneracy,
no record ambiguity.

Minesweeper currently does the opposite, calling `clearSavedState()` the
instant you lose (`MinesweeperViewModel.swift:242-250`). Klondike's stuck
banner should become an offer rather than a verdict: back up / retry this
deal / new deal.

### 3. Talkthrough pilots — two games

Per the vision: implement game-by-game, no universal assist engine.

**Nonogram (first pilot).** The solver already ships.
`Engine/NonogramLineSolver.solveLine` returns the intersection of all valid
placements and `nil` on contradiction — pure, deterministic, already
§4-compliant. It is currently only ever fed a blank grid from
`isLineSolvable`; feeding it the player's board is the feature (~40-line
adapter plus line ranking). Secondary: an on-demand "check my work" flagging
the first wrong cell in Free mode, which reuses the Lives-branch comparison.

**Sudoku (second pilot).** `SudokuCore` is already linked into the app target
but no app file imports it — it ships as dead weight, so integration is free.
Needs a public `SudokuHintEngine` returning the *first* deducible step with
technique name and unit indices, rather than `TechniqueRater`'s private
batch sweep. Rebuild `TechniqueRater.rate` on top of it so there is one
technique implementation, not two.

Graduated form for both: name the technique → highlight the region → reveal
the cell, only on request.

### 4. Cheap adds — take if the schedule holds

- **WordGrid: reveal one unfound word.** Likely the cheapest item in the
  roster. `WordGridEngine.playableWords` already enumerates findable words
  (called 121× per board generation, never at runtime). A revealed word
  scores 0, so the assist self-prices and no record semantics break.
- **FiveLetter: advisory strict mode + candidate count.** The narration
  already exists — `FiveLetterStrictValidator` emits "Keep S in spot 1" /
  "Do not use E" — today as a hard rejection. Reframe it as opt-in and
  non-blocking. Add "142 words still fit", computed by replay-consistency
  against the 1,033-word answer pool, which leaks nothing not already on
  screen.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **Any revive / lifesaver / second chance**, free or otherwise. Reasoning
  above; the arcade case was already pre-litigated in `16-CONTEXT.md`.
- **Merge undo.** The spawn happens *after* the slide using the live RNG, so
  undo-then-reswipe re-rolls it, unlimited. That is save-scumming, and Merge
  is the only non-arcade game whose record is a raw score the assist
  directly inflates. Merge's honest help is a strategy primer — and since no
  per-game how-to-play screen exists anywhere in the app, build that as a
  cross-game surface in a later milestone, not as a Merge one-off.
- **Klondike move hints.** "Any legal move" is noise (drawing from stock is
  always legal); ranking productive moves needs search Klondike cannot
  afford. Klondike gets dignity work only — undo and the banner rewrite.
- **Stack and Snake — permanently out of assist scope.** Reflex games have
  no stuck state. Kindness there is instant restart and honest records.
  Add this line to `v2.0-VISION.md:245-252` when the milestone opens.
- **FreeCell's fifth free cell**, per the vision — now with a reason:
  `maxMoveable` is `(freeCells+1) * 2^emptyCols`, so a fifth cell *doubles*
  supermove capacity and breaks deal-number comparability with every other
  FreeCell. It is a rules fork, not an assist.
- **Minesweeper — deferred to v1.7, deliberately.** It has the best feature
  the survey found: prove no deduction exists, then say so honestly ("this
  one's a guess") and offer to spend the guess. It is also the largest new
  engine (tiered constraint solving plus enumeration, ~500-600 LOC) and the
  flagship game. Related and larger: `BoardGenerator.generate` has **no
  solvability guarantee** — one uniform shuffle with a first-tap exclusion —
  so players lose to unfair boards today. The honest fix is a generator that
  does not produce them, which needs the same solver. That is a v1.7
  headline, not a v1.6 add-on.
- FreeCell "show me a move" — strong candidate (exact move oracle in
  `FreeCellRules.isLost`, hint toast surface already shipped) but a third
  pilot. Take only if Nonogram and Sudoku land early.

## Cross-cutting invariants

- **Narration must never be wrong.** A confidently-worded deduction that is
  false is worse than no assist. Where a solver reasons from player-supplied
  marks, treat them as *belief, not truth* — one wrong flag otherwise
  produces a confident wrong answer. Gate each pilot on a seeded fuzz test
  asserting every claimed-safe cell genuinely is safe.
- **Assists are unmetered.** No allowance, no pool, no reset, no counter to
  spend. A rationed hint is a currency.
- **Video Mode help stays visible without becoming an info chip.** Large-zone
  compact rows carry a lightbulb action; small-zone toolbars route it opposite
  the PiP. The 2-chip cap still applies to passive info such as lives/time.
- **Assist engines stay pure** — Foundation-only, deterministic, no SwiftUI,
  no `modelContext`, tested in the same commit, per §4 and §5.
- **Timers pause during a talkthrough.** Several games anchor elapsed time to
  wall clock, so a modal explanation silently pollutes best times.
- Assists respect haptics / SFX / animation settings and Reduce Motion, and
  are disableable wholesale.

## Decided — record semantics (2026-08-01)

The Track 0 questions are settled. Implement to these; do not reopen without
a reason that did not exist on this date.

- **An assisted win is a win, but never a record.** It counts toward games
  played, win rate, and streaks. It does not write a `BestTime` or
  `BestScore`. Rationale: the record is a claim about unaided performance,
  and the moment it stops being that it stops being worth keeping. Nothing
  else about the win is diminished.
- **An assisted puzzle is not consumed.** `wonPuzzleIDs` must not add a
  puzzle solved with help, and the same holds for daily results. Burning a
  puzzle you needed help with is a quiet punishment, and it removes the one
  puzzle a player might most want to revisit unaided.
- **Disclosure is plain and singular:** "Solved with 1 hint" on the record
  itself. No separate assisted column, no parallel leaderboard, no asterisk
  styling, no comparison to unassisted play. The player is told what
  happened, once, without being ranked for it.

## Open decisions to resolve in planning

1. **Sudoku's two-technique ceiling.** Only naked and hidden singles exist,
   so Hard/Extreme talkthroughs fall through to a bare reveal. Write copy
   that makes the fallback feel intentional, or add a third technique.
2. **Does the extra tap to restart in Stack/Snake stay?** It deviates from
   the v1.5 spec with no ADR, and is defensible as input debounce. Decide
   and write the ADR either way.
3. **Nonogram assist cost.** Placement enumeration was already the dominant
   20×20 swipe-lag source. A 40-line scan must be on-demand, off the
   mutation path — confirm on device.
4. **Talkthrough presentation** — inline banner, sheet, or toast. Affects
   the timer-pause decision and Video Mode degradation.
