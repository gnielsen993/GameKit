## Decisions for later plans

- **A1 FAIL, plan 02 and 05:** rounded type comes from `RoundedType.font(_:weight:)` returning `Font.system(style, design: .rounded, weight:)` (Dynamic Type scaling verified at AccessibilityXXXL). Never put `.fontDesign(.rounded)` on a container or on a text that uses `monoNumber`; it turns the monospaced timer proportional.
- **A8 PASS, plan 05:** the win time is `Font.system(.largeTitle, design: .rounded, weight: .heavy).monospacedDigit()`; no fixed-width frame.
- **A2 PASS, plan 02 Task 3:** `PieceButtonStyle` reads the debug strength with `@AppStorage` under `#if DEBUG`; no `EnvironmentKey` at the app root.
- **A3 PASS, plan 03:** counter-triggered per-cell `KeyframeAnimator` (hold by step x 0.045 s, rise 0.245 s, fall 0.455 s, scale 1.08) is the wave and win-sweep mechanism; cost is fine in a Debug build (max frame gap 18 ms over 81 cells).
- **A4 PASS, plan 02:** the RESEARCH.md construction order with `.contentShape(Rectangle())` last; no enlarged hit region.
- **A6 PASS, plan 06 Task 2:** automate Reduce Motion with the simctl `defaults write` plus an app relaunch, and write `-bool false` afterwards.
