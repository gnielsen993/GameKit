# Phase 19.1: Design Language: Pieces - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-11-26
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Turn the approved `DESIGN.md` §0 language into shared, tested primitives and
prove it in one shipped game. When this phase is done, a Sudoku session from
opening the game to winning it reads the way sketch 012 does, on a real device,
and Gabe has picked the strength of the piece.

In scope: the piece primitive; its use for Sudoku's number pad, erase, the
Value/Notes control and the primary buttons on Sudoku's end card; stronger
answers to a placement, to a completed row, column or box, and to a win; a
debug-only strength switch; the `DESIGN.md` §3 specs.

Not in scope: Home, Library, the mini-board component, any game other than
Sudoku, Stats, onboarding, DesignKit changes.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Rollout to the other ten games
- **D-01:** Sudoku is the only game converted in this phase. A new Phase 20.1 after Home converts the remaining ten.
- **D-02:** Nothing in the pieces language ships to players until all eleven games match. No release carries one converted game among ten unconverted ones. The `2.0` line stays unshipped until then; a 1.x slice may only ship work that does not expose the new look. — **Reversibility:** costly — relaxing it later means shipping a visibly inconsistent app, and tightening it later is free.

### Settling the feel
- **D-03:** Build a debug-only switch with three strengths, Subtle, Medium and Bold, that changes the piece's edge depth, press travel and spring together. Gabe plays Sudoku with each on his phone and picks one. Not sliders, not a single fixed guess.
- **D-04:** The switch never reaches a release build. After Gabe picks, the chosen values become the constants, the other two are deleted, and `DESIGN.md` §3 records the numbers.
- **D-05:** Starting values come from sketch 012: edge about 4 to 6pt, press travel nearly the full edge depth, a spring that overshoots slightly on release. Medium should match the sketch; Subtle and Bold bracket it clearly enough to feel different.

### Typeface
- **D-06:** Rounded type for titles, pad keys and primary buttons; regular for body text, captions, Stats and Settings. As in sketch 012.
- **D-07:** Done inside GameDrawer only. DesignKit's typography tokens are shared with HabitTracker and FitnessTracker and are not changed. — **Reversibility:** costly — putting it in DesignKit later touches every consumer; keeping it local now costs one helper.

### Carried forward (already decided, do not reopen)
- **D-08:** `DESIGN.md` §0 rules 1 to 5 and its "what this is not" paragraph: no praise callouts, no particles on ordinary moves, no screen shake.
- **D-09:** Assisted wins count as wins and set no record (v1.6). The win sheet names a personal best only when one was actually set.
- **D-10:** Every delight layer is gated by the existing haptics, SFX and animations settings and by Reduce Motion (CLAUDE.md §1). With motion off, pieces keep shape and edge and moves still register visibly.
- **D-11:** The piece is the same on every preset. It is not a Classic-only skin, so the Classic restomod policy is unaffected.

### Claude's Discretion
- Where the piece lives: `Core/`, beside `SurfaceDepth.swift` and `PressableButtonStyle.swift`. Promote to DesignKit only after a second app wants it (CLAUDE.md §4).
- How the piece is expressed (a `ButtonStyle`, a view modifier, or both), and whether it replaces or wraps `PressableButtonStyle`.
- Edge color derivation per preset, provided it comes from theme tokens plus the lighting helpers in `SurfaceDepth.swift` and no color literal appears in a game view.
- `SudokuEndStateCard` uses DesignKit's `DKButton`. Do not restyle `DKButton`; give Sudoku a GameDrawer-local piece button for its primary and secondary actions.
- Exact timing and stagger of the unit-complete sequence, and the count-up on the win sheet.
- The mini-board component moved to Phase 20, since nothing in the Sudoku loop uses it.
- No new wrong-move copy. Sketch 012 showed a sentence explaining a wrong number; the shipped game has no honest way to say why in every case, so keep the existing wrong-entry behavior and make it land harder.

</decisions>

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

Sudoku already has a quiet version of most of this. The phase strengthens and
unifies; it does not start from zero.

### Reusable Assets
- `gamekit/gamekit/Core/PressableButtonStyle.swift`: press scale (0.94) with spring, already gated on Reduce Motion and the animations setting. The piece's press behavior belongs here or beside it.
- `gamekit/gamekit/Core/SurfaceDepth.swift`: `raisedSheen`, `chipShadow()`, `activeGlow`. The only place lighting literals are allowed.
- `gamekit/gamekit/Core/MotionGate.swift`: `feedbackAnimation(_:value:)`, the gated animation helper.
- `gamekit/gamekit/Core/Haptics.swift`: AHAP playback gated on the haptics setting.
- `gamekit/gamekit/Games/Sudoku/SudokuViewModel+CompletionFeedback.swift`: already computes completed row, column and box indices (`completionGlowIndices`) and the digit-used-up pulse (`justCompletedDigit`).
- `gamekit/gamekit/Games/Sudoku/SudokuNumberPad.swift`: the nine keys, currently a flat surface with a 1pt border and `chipShadow()`.
- `gamekit/gamekit/Games/Sudoku/SudokuEndStateCard.swift`: win, game-over and practice-complete card built on `DKCard` and `DKButton`.

### Established Patterns
- Game views take `theme` and read tokens only; the pre-commit hook rejects color literals, numeric `cornerRadius:` and `.padding()` in `Games/` and `Screens/`.
- View-model feedback is counter-triggered (`completionGlowCount`, `numberCompleteCount`); views react with `onChange`. Keep that shape.
- Files stay under 400 lines; `SudokuViewModel.swift` (696) and several others are on the size baseline and may not grow.
- Sudoku has a Video Mode twin, `SudokuGameView+VideoMode.swift`, that lays out the same controls. Both paths must use the piece.

### Integration Points
- Pad keys, erase and the Value/Notes control in `SudokuGameView.swift` and `SudokuGameView+VideoMode.swift`.
- Cell-level landing and unit-complete rendering in `SudokuCellView.swift` and `SudokuBoardView.swift`.
- The debug strength switch: there is no `#if DEBUG` section in Settings today; add one that is compiled out of release.
- Tests live in `gamekit/gamekitTests/` (`Core/`, `Games/`); pure logic added here ships with tests in the same commit.

</code_context>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Gabe's brief, in his words: "less is more, but we cannot go nothing at all", and the app needs "life", meaning what makes successful apps land, not movement for its own sake.
- He named Block Blast as a reference for how an app should hit. Take its principles (instant play, an answer to every action, a rewarding end), not its skin.
- He liked sketch 011 C for its look and 011 A for its aliveness, and approved their combination in sketch 012 as "for sure the direction, this looks real good".
- The win in the sketch: the word "Solved", the time large and counting up, a small "Your best Hard time" badge, "Next puzzle" as the primary piece, "Back to the drawer" as the secondary.

</specifics>
