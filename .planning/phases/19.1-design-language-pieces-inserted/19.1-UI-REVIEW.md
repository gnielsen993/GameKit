# Phase 19.1 — UI Review

**Audited:** 2026-11-27
**Baseline:** DESIGN.md section 0 (pieces language), section 3.8 (piece spec), sections 2, 3.4, 3.6, 4, 9, 10, and 19.1-CONTEXT.md. No UI-SPEC.md by decision D-21.
**Screenshots:** not captured by this audit. Native SwiftUI app, so no dev server and no Playwright. Evidence is 24 UI-test screenshots from the locked build (iPhone 17 simulator), of which 16 were opened: pad on Classic light/dark, Cream, Voltage light/dark, Dracula; win card on Classic light/dark, Voltage light/dark, Dracula; lives-wrong-move; erase-and-mode; motion-off-placement; win-card-motion-off. Contrast figures below were measured from those PNGs.
**Interaction captures:** off (workflow.ui_interaction_capture is false)

Limits of this audit, stated once:

- Screenshots are static. Press travel, spring, the placement pop, the unit and win ripples, the count-up, the wrong-cell shake and every haptic were not seen and are not scored. Where a finding touches them it is from code.
- Not in the screenshot set, so not seen: all four Video Mode layouts (compact pieces), iPhone SE, the largest text size, the game-over and practice-complete cards, a pressed piece in any motion setting, an assisted win (hint disclosure subtitle), and a win without a best (no badge).
- The gitignore gate was not run: nothing was captured, and the orchestrator asked that no file other than this one be touched.
- No registry audit: no components.json, not a shadcn project.

## Top 3 Priority Fixes

1. **The win time is not large** (WARNING) — DESIGN.md section 0 rule 5 says finishing "shows the result large", and CONTEXT calls for "the time large and counting up". `RoundedType.winTime` is `.largeTitle` (34pt) at `gamekit/gamekit/Core/RoundedType.swift:27`, and the title above it is `titleLarge` (32pt bold rounded) at `gamekit/gamekit/Games/Sudoku/SudokuEndStateCard.swift:59`. In every win screenshot "Solved" and "4:23" are the same visual size, so the card has two competing headlines and the count-up, the one moving thing, has no room to read as the event. Fix: make the time clearly the largest element, for example a rounded heavy font near 56pt that still scales (`Font.system(size:)` does not scale, so use a `@ScaledMetric` relative to `.largeTitle`), or drop the title to `theme.typography.title`. Update the section 4 row in the same change. The size is a look decision, so it wants a device look before it is fixed.

2. **The Value/Notes pill is one toggle, not two segments** (WARNING) — `gamekit/gamekit/Games/Sudoku/SudokuModePill.swift:33-35` wraps both segments in a single `Button` that calls `onSelect(toggledMode)`, so tapping "Value" while Value is already active switches to Notes. Lines 52-55 expose it to VoiceOver as one element labelled "Toggle mode". DESIGN.md section 3.4 and section 9 require each segment to be `.isButton` with `.isSelected` on the active one. This behavior is carried from 1.6 (the base file has the same button), but the file was rebuilt as a piece this phase and it is the pattern Phase 20.1 will copy into the other mode pills. Fix: keep one capsule piece for the depth, and inside it make each segment its own accessibility element that selects its own mode (tap on the active segment does nothing), or use `accessibilityRepresentation` with two buttons.

3. **On Classic, a wrong digit and a correct digit are almost the same red** (WARNING) — `gamekit/gamekit/Games/Sudoku/SudokuCellView.swift:146-148` colors the player's digits `accentPrimary` and incorrect ones `danger`. Measured on Classic light: accent is rgb(192, 57, 43), danger is rgb(220, 38, 38). In the motion-off-placement screenshot the wrong "1" is only distinguishable from the player's other digits by a slight hue shift. Classic is the default preset, and Free mode relies on this color alone. Carried from 1.6, but section 0 rule 3 ("a rejected move says why") and section 10.4 (meaning must not rest on one channel) both apply to the surface this phase rebuilt. Fix: add a non-color mark to an incorrect digit, for example a `danger` wash at about 12% behind it or a short underline, in the `.user` case at lines 141-152.

## Detailed Findings

### Pillar 1: Copywriting (3/4)

Matches the contract: "Solved", the difficulty subtitle, "Your best Easy time", "Next puzzle", "Back to the drawer", "View board" (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:79, 125, 136, 150, 174`). No praise, countdown, streak or purchase copy anywhere in the card. The badge is gated on `record?.setBestTime == true` (line 108), so the claim is truthful.

- WARNING: casing is inconsistent for the same destination. The card says "Back to the drawer" (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:79`); the toolbar back button's label is "Back to The Drawer" (`SudokuGameView.swift:334`), which is what DESIGN.md section 9 lists. The resume dialog says "New Puzzle" in title case (`SudokuGameView.swift:89`) while the card says "Next puzzle".
- WARNING: an assisted win reads "Solved" then "Easy · Solved with 1 hint" (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:162-164`). The word repeats one line apart. "Easy · 1 hint used" says the same thing. Not seen in a screenshot.
- WARNING: "You used all 3 lives." hard-codes the count (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:166`). Carried from 1.6; wrong the day `livesPerPuzzle` changes.
- Minor: the pill's VoiceOver label "Toggle mode" (`SudokuModePill.swift:53`) names a mechanism, not the control. Folded into priority fix 2.

### Pillar 2: Visuals (2/4)

What holds: on all six preset screenshots the pad key, the pill and erase read as a face over a lower edge, the selected key fills with the accent over a darker edge, and exhausted keys sit flat with a strike, as section 3.8 specifies. The pill and pad are the clear focal controls under the board. The piece is the same construction on every preset; there is no per-preset branch in `PieceButtonStyle.swift`.

- WARNING: the win time is not the large element. See priority fix 1. This is the main reason for the score: the win card is half of what the phase shipped and its result does not lead.
- WARNING (already flagged to Gabe, with a new measurement): the neutral "Back to the drawer" face is the same color as the card on every preset, not only dark ones. Measured face-to-card contrast is 1.0:1 on Classic light, Classic dark, Dracula and Voltage dark, because both are `theme.colors.surface` (`PieceCTAButton.swift:58` on a `DKCard`, `SudokuEndStateCard.swift:42`). On light presets the contact shadow rescues the shape; on dark presets the shadow is invisible and the button reads as a label over a grey bar (edge-to-card about 2.2 to 2.3:1). A piece is supposed to be a solid cap; here the cap has no sides.
- WARNING: on Classic light, the default preset, the neutral edge is the faintest of any preset checked. Measured edge-to-background: 1.58:1 on Classic light against 2.44 to 2.76:1 on Classic dark, Dracula and Voltage dark. The pad key face-to-background is 1.07 to 1.21:1 everywhere, so the edge is the only thing that makes a key a piece, and it is weakest where most players will see it. The constant is `SurfaceDepth.pieceNeutralEdgeInkOpacity = 0.28` at `gamekit/gamekit/Core/SurfaceDepth.swift:50`. Gabe picked Medium on device, so this is a measurement to weigh, not a call to change it.
- WARNING: the exhausted-key strike is centered on the whole key, while the digit sits in the upper half above an empty count row (`SudokuNumberPad.swift:55, 66-68`). In the near-win screenshots eight of nine keys show a slash that starts above-left of the digit and runs off into the blank lower half. It reads as slightly misregistered, and in the late game it is most of the pad.
- Minor: disabled keys sit one edge depth (4pt) lower than live keys, so the top line of the pad steps. This is section 3.8 as written ("sit flat on their edge"); noted because it is visible, not because it is wrong.
- Already flagged, not rescored: the centered win card against Video Mode's flat rows (not seen here; no Video Mode screenshot).
- Outside this phase: about 90pt of empty band above and below the board on iPhone 17 leaves the timer floating far from the board. Layout is unchanged from 1.6.

### Pillar 3: Color (3/4)

Token discipline is clean. In the five converted view files and the three `Core/Piece*` files there is no hex, no `Color(` literal and no `rgb(`. The only color literals are the three lighting constants in `Core/SurfaceDepth.swift:44-53`, which is where section 3.0 says they belong. Edge colors follow section 3.8 exactly: `surface` plus `textPrimary` at 28% for neutral, fill plus black 32% for filled (`PieceButtonStyle.swift:108-121`). Active pill text and primary button text use `background` on accent and read on every preset checked (Classic light primary label 4.82:1).

- WARNING: wrong digit versus own digit on Classic. See priority fix 3.
- WARNING: the best badge is accent text on a 14% accent capsule (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:127-130`). Measured 4.4:1 on Classic light for bold caption text, just under 4.5:1. Dark presets are comfortably above.
- WARNING: accent is doing three jobs on the win card: the time, the badge and the primary button. Section 2.2 says accent is the player's active choice and "not a generic highlight for informational callouts". Section 4 explicitly assigns accent to the win time, so that one is contract; the badge is not covered by any row. Either add it to section 2 or section 3.6, or move the badge to `success`, which section 2 defines as the win color.
- Minor: opacity literals on tokens in view code: `background.opacity(0.8)` and `textSecondary.opacity(0.55)` (`SudokuNumberPad.swift:58, 71`), `accentPrimary.opacity(0.14)` (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:130`), `0.22` and `0.24` in `SudokuCellView.swift:127, 186`. The hook allows them and most predate the phase; they are still unnamed numbers.
- The win shows a `success` border and wash on the board (`SudokuBoardView.swift:164-177`) while the win wave fills in `accentPrimary`. Two colors for one event; both are within section 2 and section 10.2. Not seen in motion.

### Pillar 4: Typography (3/4)

Rounded type is applied where section 3.8 and D-06 say and nowhere else: pad digits (`SudokuNumberPad.swift:49`), pill labels (`SudokuModePill.swift:67-69`), the card title (DesignKit's `titleLarge` is already rounded), the win time, the badge and the two piece buttons (`PieceCTAButton.swift:45`). Body, caption, the subtitle, "View board" and the timer stay regular. The rounded helper is an explicit font at the label, so the monospaced timer is untouched, as spike A1 required. The win time carries `.monospacedDigit()` (`SudokuCountUpTime.swift:59`).

- WARNING: size hierarchy on the win card. Title 32pt, time 34pt. See priority fix 1.
- WARNING: five weights on one small surface: regular (compact pill label, body), medium (erase glyph, `SudokuEraseButton.swift:22`), semibold (pill label and glyphs), bold (pad digits, buttons, badge, title), heavy (win time). The erase glyph at medium sits beside the pill's glyphs at semibold in the same row; the compact pill label is regular while the full one is semibold (`SudokuModePill.swift:68-69`).
- Minor: the board's given digits are `title` bold and the pad digits are rounded `title2` bold. Same size, two designs, by decision D-06 (rounded for keys, not the board). Noted so Phase 20.1 does not treat it as an accident.
- Minor, DesignKit and out of scope: `titleLarge` is a fixed 32pt (`TypographyTokens.swift:12`), so "Solved" does not scale with Dynamic Type while everything under it does.
- Not seen: pad digits have no `lineLimit` or `minimumScaleFactor` (`SudokuNumberPad.swift:48-49`) inside keys about 37pt wide. Plan 06's largest-text test asserts on-screen and tappable, not legible, and there is no largest-text screenshot in the set.

### Pillar 5: Spacing (3/4)

Every padding and stack spacing in the converted views reads a `theme.spacing` token: pad `xs` gaps and `m` margins, key `s` vertical padding, pill `xs` wrapper with `l`/`s` segments, card `l` rhythm with `s` between actions, `xl` and `m` outer margins. Radii are `theme.radii.chip` for keys and `theme.radii.button` for the buttons and compact erase. The card's vertical rhythm is even in the screenshots and top and bottom margins balance. The edge is reserved with bottom padding (`PieceButtonStyle.swift:77`), so pieces do not overlap their neighbors and the hit region is the un-offset frame.

- WARNING: erase is a literal 36 by 36pt face (`SudokuEraseButton.swift:25-26`), so its hit region is 36 by 40pt with the edge, under the 44pt the rest of the document uses (section 6, and the pill's own 44pt segments). Carried from 1.6, but it is now the smallest piece on a screen whose premise is that pieces are solid things to press, and it sits alone at the trailing edge. Widening the label frame to 44 and keeping the glyph size would fix it without moving the row.
- WARNING: remaining literals in the converted files: `VStack(spacing: 2)` (`SudokuNumberPad.swift:47`), `lineWidth: 1.5` (line 72), `minHeight: 44` (`SudokuModePill.swift:78`, `SudokuEndStateCard.swift:139`), glyph sizes 13 and 16. The 44s and glyph sizes are in section 3.4; the 2 and 36 are not in any spec.
- Minor: pad keys are about 37pt wide on iPhone 17 (nine across 370pt). That is the nine-key constraint from section 5.5, unchanged, and height is comfortable.
- Not seen: compact piece spacing in the Video Mode rows.

### Pillar 6: Experience Design (3/4)

State coverage from code: loading ("Loading puzzle…", `SudokuGameView.swift:229-233`), resume choice card, win, game over, practice complete, disabled pieces (flat, dimmed, struck where exhausted), and non-interactive pad at 40%. Motion is gated consistently on `animationsEnabled && !reduceMotion` in the piece (`PieceButtonStyle.swift:56`), the cell (`SudokuBoardView.swift:65`), the count-up (`SudokuCountUpTime.swift:25`) and the pre-roll (`SudokuGameView.swift:97, 110`), each with a static fallback: pressed fill, 22% glow, final time at once, no pre-roll. The motion-off screenshots confirm the placement registers and the card shows its final time. Haptics hang off the board and are gated on `hapticsEnabled` only, independent of animations, as section 8 requires. The win card scrolls when it cannot fit (`SudokuEndStateCard.swift:45-49`), VoiceOver hears the final time, not a number in flight, and pad keys keep "Place N, M remaining". The primary action after a win is the next game; no confirmation is inserted. Lives mode rejects a wrong digit without showing it (lives-wrong-move screenshot: cell empty, two hearts).

- WARNING: mode pill accessibility and tap behavior. See priority fix 2.
- WARNING: wrong digit distinguishable by color only on the default preset. See priority fix 3.
- WARNING: erase is enabled whenever the game is interactive (`SudokuEraseButton.swift:34`), including when the selected cell is a given or empty, so a live piece can sink and do nothing. Section 0 rule 3 says every move answers back; a piece with nothing to do should sit flat.
- WARNING, documented in DESIGN.md section 3.8 as unsettled and not new: the winning move can stack four haptics (placement, unit, digit used up, win) at `SudokuGameView.swift:272-291`, which section 8.2 forbids, and the milestone runs at 0.85 where section 8.2 lists 1.0.
- Already flagged to Gabe, noted and not rescored: frame cost on the pad and the ripples was never measured (81 keyframe animators run on every wrong move and every unit, `SudokuCellView.swift:55-70`); the pressed key was observed to differ between Reduce Motion and Animations off although the code path is one expression; win ripple peak strength.
- Not verified by this audit: anything in motion, the game-over and practice-complete cards, Video Mode, iPhone SE, the largest text size.

## Other recommendations (after the top three)

1. Give the neutral secondary button a face that differs from the card it sits on, on every preset (measured 1.0:1 today).
2. Weigh the Classic light neutral edge (1.58:1) against the dark presets (2.4 to 2.8:1) at the next device look.
3. Widen the erase target to 44pt and disable it when there is nothing to erase.
4. Lift the best badge to at least 4.5:1 on Classic light, and record its color in DESIGN.md.
5. Register the exhausted-key strike on the digit, not on the whole key.
6. Settle casing: "the drawer" or "The Drawer"; "New Puzzle" or "Next puzzle".
7. Reword the assisted subtitle so "Solved" does not repeat.
8. Add `lineLimit(1)` and a `minimumScaleFactor` to pad digits, then capture a largest-text screenshot.
9. Bring the erase glyph to semibold to match the pill glyphs beside it.

Items 1 and 2 and priority fix 1 are look decisions for Gabe, not gaps to close blind. The rest can be built.
