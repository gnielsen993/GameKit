  <how-to-verify>
Getting it on your phone (skip to "What to try" if Claude says it is already installed):
1. Plug your iPhone into the Mac with a cable and unlock it. If it asks "Trust this computer?", tap Trust.
2. Xcode is open on the Mac. At the top of the Xcode window, next to the word "gamekit", click the device name and choose your iPhone from the list.
3. Click the Play button (the triangle at the top left), or press Command-R. The first time, your iPhone may ask you to turn on Developer Mode (Settings, Privacy and Security, Developer Mode, then restart) and to trust the developer (Settings, General, VPN and Device Management). Then press Play again.
4. GameDrawer opens on your phone. This build replaces the App Store version on the phone; your saved games and stats stay. The near-win shortcut below never writes to your stats or saved games.

What to try (about 15 minutes):
1. Open Sudoku and start a puzzle. Tap the sliders button at the top right. Under "Debug" you can pick Subtle, Medium or Bold. The same switch is at the bottom of Settings.
2. At each strength, play for a minute: place numbers, use erase, switch between Value and Notes. Notice: does a key feel like a physical button that goes down under your thumb and comes back up? Is it too stiff, too soft, too deep, too shallow? Does a number "land" when you place it?
3. Finish a row, a column or a box. Does the ripple feel like a reward, and is it still pleasant the tenth time, or is it noisy?
4. Switch the puzzle to Lives mode (same sliders menu) and enter a wrong number on purpose. The square should shake and you lose a heart; the wrong number is not shown.
5. In the sliders menu tap "Three cells from a win" and fill the three empty squares with the only number left on the keypad. Look at the win: "Solved", the time counting up, the "Your best ... time" badge (this shortcut always shows it so you can see it), "Next puzzle" and "Back to the drawer".
6. Two places differ from the sketch on purpose; tell me if either bothers you: the win appears as a card in the middle of the screen, not a sheet sliding up from the bottom (so it works the same with a video playing beside the game); and in Video Mode's top or bottom band layout, back, restart, hint and the menu stay flat while erase and Value/Notes are pieces.
7. Also new: a filled row that contains a wrong number no longer lights up or buzzes (only correct rows are celebrated).
8. Colors: in Settings, try the Classic, Dracula, Voltage and Cream themes. Do the keys still read as solid buttons on each?
9. Motion: on the iPhone, turn on Settings, Accessibility, Motion, Reduce Motion, and play a few moves; then turn it off again. Separately, turn off Animations in GameDrawer's Settings. In both cases the keys should keep their shape and edge, nothing should move or ripple, but every move should still show (a number appears, a finished row glows briefly, a wrong square flashes).
10. If Claude asks, keep the phone plugged in while you tap numbers for 20 seconds so it can record a smoothness trace.

Claude records in the plan SUMMARY: the strength you pick, any number changes you ask for, your answers on the centered card and the Video Mode row, and the frame-check result if it was taken on the phone.
