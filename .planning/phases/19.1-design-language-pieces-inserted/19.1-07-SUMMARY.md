Copy the sections below into the new file then, in the order of `Docs/releases/TEMPLATE.md`.

### Summary
- The pieces design language: everything you press is a solid piece with a visible lower edge that sinks under the finger and springs back. Proven first in Sudoku in Phase 19.1. It does not ship until all eleven games match (D-02, Phase 20.1).

### User-facing changes
- Sudoku's number pad, erase and Value/Notes are pieces, in every layout including Video Mode.
- Placed digits pop in. A correctly completed row, column or box ripples out from the move. A wrong move in Lives mode shakes that cell. The win ripples the whole board.
- The win card shows Solved, the time counting up, a personal-best badge only when the win really set a best, Next puzzle as the primary action and Back to the drawer.
- Rounded type for pad keys, titles and primary buttons.
- All of it follows the Animations setting and Reduce Motion: with motion off, pieces keep their shape and edge, nothing travels or ripples, and moves still register at once.

### Internal changes
- `PieceSpec`, `PieceButtonStyle`, `PieceCTAButton` and `RoundedType` in `Core/`. `PieceSpec.current` is the single set of piece constants (Medium, picked on device 2026-11-27).
- `GameStats` reports whether a win set a best through `RecordResult`.
- `SudokuViewModel` split into `+Outcome` and `+TestSeam`, with a tighter size ratchet.
- DEBUG near-win fixture and the `--sudoku-near-win` launch argument, absent from Release.

### Fixes
- The Sudoku completion glow ran backwards and ignored the animations setting.
- A full row, column or box that holds a wrong digit is no longer celebrated (D-18).
- The wrong-move shake that DESIGN.md described now exists.

### Risks / notes
- Not shippable on its own: Sudoku is the only converted game (D-02). Phase 20.1 converts the other ten.
- Haptic stacking on the winning move is unchanged from 1.6 (placement, unit, digit used up and win can fire together).
- Frame cost of the pad and the ripples was not measured on a device in Phase 19.1.
