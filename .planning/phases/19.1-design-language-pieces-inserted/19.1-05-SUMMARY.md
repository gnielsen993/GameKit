# Phase 19.1 Plan 05: Win Card, Counting Time and Debug Menu Summary

**Winning a Sudoku now shows Solved, a large rounded time that counts up (or sits at its final value with motion off), a best-time badge only when GameStats reports a best, and Next puzzle / Back to the drawer as large pieces inside the same centered card, with a Debug-only menu section for the piece strengths and a one-tap near-win.**

## Accomplishments

- `SudokuWinTime` and `SudokuCountUpTime`: pure format and cubic ease-out (0.3s delay, 1.1s), a gated `TimelineView` count-up over a hidden final-string layout, VoiceOver always reads the final time. `RoundedType.winTime` is largeTitle heavy rounded with `.monospacedDigit()` (A1 FAIL path for the font, A8 PASS for the digits).
- `PieceCTAButton` (Core): primary accent-filled and secondary neutral large pieces; DKButton and DesignKit untouched (DesignKit still `4ab0447`, clean).
- `SudokuEndStateCard`: won, game-over and practice-complete variants; `record` is passed only for `.won`; the badge exists only under `record?.setBestTime == true`. Two new catalog keys ("Back to the drawer", "Your best %@ time") added by hand because a build does not write them.
- DEBUG settings menu section (strength picker, Three cells from a win) at all three menu call sites; Release strings check `control=4`, `debug=0`.
- DESIGN.md (on main, merged into 2.0): 3.6 Sudoku exception and win content, 3.8 large size and contact shadow, 4 win-time row, 10.2 count-up row.

## Contact shadow review (asked for at dispatch)

I looked at the end card on Classic with the plan 02 shadow. It was wrong: a blurred shadow (radius 6, y = depth + 3) cast from a face-position layer showed as a grey halo around the whole button, up the sides and above the top edge, and it smeared into the gap between Next puzzle and Back to the drawer. The edge itself (dark red under the primary, grey under the secondary) was correct and clearly visible.

Fix (commit `bb2e29b`): the shadow is now cast by the offset edge layer, radius 3, y 2, black 22%. Re-viewed on Classic (light), Dracula (dark) and Voltage (dark): the shadow now reads as a tight contact under the edge with no halo or smear. On the two dark presets it is invisible against the dark card, which is expected; the lighter edge carries the depth.

Remaining observation, not changed: on dark presets the neutral "Back to the drawer" face is nearly the card's own colour, so the button is defined mostly by its edge. It reads, but it is quieter than on light presets. Flagged for the device review.
