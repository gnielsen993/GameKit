## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Unit ripple blanked cells for about 200 ms**
- **Found during:** Task 1, 70-screenshot sweep
- **Issue:** at the start of every unit ripple, cells outside the unit and the origin cell rendered blank for about 200 ms, caused by a zero-length leading hold keyframe.
- **Fix:** `waveHold(step:)` floors at 1 ms. Proven with 70-screenshot sweeps before (4 blank frames) and after (none). New unit test `waveHoldIsNeverZero`.
- **Files modified:** `SudokuCellFeedback.swift`, `SudokuCompletionWaveTests.swift`
- **Commit:** `5a8fbbf`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] Three layout failures at AccessibilityXXXL**
- **Found during:** Task 1, `testLargestTextSize`
- **Issue:** the Value/Notes pill overran Erase; "Back to the drawer" truncated; the win card was taller than the iPhone SE screen.
- **Fix:** pill Dynamic Type capped at accessibility1 (compact at xxxLarge); button labels wrap (lineLimit 3, scale 0.8); the card uses ViewThatFits with a ScrollView fallback. On SE at XXXL the secondary action and View board need a scroll.
- **Files modified:** `PieceCTAButton.swift`, `SudokuModePill.swift`, `SudokuEndStateCard.swift`
- **Commit:** `69bf9ec`
